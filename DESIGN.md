# QuestKeeps existing visual system

The shared identity also appears in verification emails, Telegram bot imagery/copy, app icons, social previews and bilingual README screenshots. Consult [the brand surface inventory](docs/BRAND-SURFACES.md) during every redesign; external uploads do not update automatically when source assets change.

This is an extension of the implemented app, not a redesign. app/theme.css is the token authority. Preserve Cobalt Clear and Night Quest appearances, the local Onest typeface, QK identity, established button sizes and focus treatment.

Profile and friends are Operate surfaces: clear identity, explicit request states, quiet row lists, readable privacy boundaries, responsive columns. About is a Read surface: a warm invitation into shared travel memories, with real product screenshots and concise privacy facts. Avoid step-by-step onboarding on About. The screenshot stack follows the user-requested perspective composition, with hover/focus straightening, a mobile swipe strip and reduced-motion support. No stock testimonials, fabricated product metrics or decorative illustration. Product screenshots may use explicitly labeled fictional people and trips; never publish private user data.

Responsive density uses three reference viewports: 393×852 (phone), 1024×1366 (tablet) and 1440×900 (laptop). Archive side gutters are 4px on phones and 8px on larger screens, respecting safe areas, without a maximum page width; gallery gaps match those gutters. Gallery density is two, three and four columns respectively; virtual feeds read their geometry from CSS and preserve the visible frame when column counts change. On phones, trip sections form a horizontal strip and place suggestions show one readable card plus a glimpse of the next. Confirmed places use a compact text/photo row; on phones, text and imagery each occupy 50% of the card and images are contained without cropping. Keep touch controls at least 44px and retain both languages and appearances. The regression matrix is in e2e/responsive-layout.spec.ts; browser emulation does not replace a real Safari/device check.

The phone header is one 64px row: menu at the left, centered QK/QuestKeeps identity, invitation bell and profile at the right. Secondary navigation, language, theme and sign-out live in the menu. Invitation notifications use an anchored non-modal native popover on every viewport; no backdrop dimming, page scroll lock or focus trap. Keep close, outside-press and Escape dismissal, keyboard return to the trigger, 44px targets, and viewport-clamped popup bounds.

Photo galleries use a stable native scroll track with virtualized cells and retained loaded previews. Do not compensate normal scrolling with scrollBy: it fights touch momentum and scrollbar dragging. Only preserve the frame when column geometry changes. Media opens edge to edge in a full-viewport viewer, with one 64px toolbar row (title and pencil, reload and close), contained uncropped imagery, and metadata below the first screen. Keep the viewer opaque and stationary on opening.
