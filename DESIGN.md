# QuestKeeps existing visual system

The shared identity also appears in verification emails, Telegram bot imagery/copy, app icons, social previews and bilingual README screenshots. Consult [the brand surface inventory](docs/BRAND-SURFACES.md) during every redesign; external uploads do not update automatically when source assets change.

This is an extension of the implemented app, not a redesign. app/theme.css is the token authority. Preserve Cobalt Clear and Night Quest appearances, the local Onest typeface, QK identity, established button sizes and focus treatment.

Profile and friends are Operate surfaces: clear identity, explicit request states, quiet row lists, readable privacy boundaries, responsive columns. About is a Read surface: a warm invitation into shared travel memories, with real product screenshots and concise privacy facts. Avoid step-by-step onboarding on About. The screenshot stack follows the user-requested perspective composition, with hover/focus straightening, a mobile swipe strip and reduced-motion support. No stock testimonials, fabricated product metrics or decorative illustration. Product screenshots may use explicitly labeled fictional people and trips; never publish private user data.

Responsive density uses three reference viewports: 393×852 (phone), 1024×1366 (tablet) and 1440×900 (laptop). Desktop archives use the full viewport width with 5vw side gutters and no maximum-width cap; tablet gutters remain 32px. On phones, travel cards and the originals-storage note retain 4px outer gutters; standalone dashboard text and actions use 16px total inset, and the single new-trip button is centered. Gallery gaps are 4px on phones and 8px on larger screens. Gallery density is two, three and four columns respectively; virtual feeds read their geometry from CSS and preserve the visible frame when column counts change. On phones, trip sections form a horizontal strip and place suggestions show one readable card plus a glimpse of the next. Confirmed places use a compact text/photo row; on phones, text and imagery each occupy 50% of the card and images are contained without cropping. Keep touch controls at least 44px and retain both languages and appearances. The regression matrix is in e2e/responsive-layout.spec.ts; browser emulation does not replace a real Safari/device check.

The phone header is one 64px row: menu at the left, centered QK/QuestKeeps identity, invitation bell and profile at the right. Secondary navigation, language, theme and sign-out live in the menu. Invitation notifications use an anchored non-modal native popover on every viewport; no backdrop dimming, page scroll lock or focus trap. Keep close, outside-press and Escape dismissal, keyboard return to the trigger, 44px targets, and viewport-clamped popup bounds.

Photo galleries use a stable native scroll track with virtualized cells and retained loaded previews. Do not compensate normal scrolling with scrollBy: it fights touch momentum and scrollbar dragging. Only preserve the frame when column geometry changes. Media opens edge to edge in a full-viewport viewer, with one 64px toolbar row (title and pencil, reload and close), contained uncropped imagery, and metadata below the first screen. Keep the viewer opaque and stationary on opening.

Place feeds use natural document flow with a consistent 16px gap between cards of different heights. Viewport proximity controls image and description loading, never card coordinates or scroll position. The phone back-to-top control is 64px and uses native smooth scrolling; reduced-motion users return immediately.

Sparse desktop dashboards (zero trips or one three-card row) use a compact vertical composition: inline empty friends, a horizontal empty archive, height-aware travel artwork and smaller section spacing. The content, header and footer fit standard laptop/desktop heights down to 720px without clipping or disabling scrolling; additional content and zoom can grow naturally. Mobile composition and the future trip carousel are separate concerns.

The fullscreen photo toolbar keeps title/pencil on the left, 90-degree rotation controls centered, and reload/close on the right. Gallery navigation overlays the frame without reducing image width. The red outlined archive-removal action appears in metadata, with explicit confirmation that the original remains on Disk.

The unified notification bell uses a red unread dot and a single short ring on a new event, with reduced-motion support. Guest mode retains signed-in profile controls; its compact badge gets a dedicated mobile header row. Guest-link copy success transitions to green for 1.5 seconds before returning to the normal action. Profile emails occupy their own full-width identity row; search and link-invitation controls share a baseline.

Publication cards are another brand surface: `lib/share-cards.ts` draws the existing QK monogram and Night Quest palette into 1080 × 1350 posts or 1080 × 1920 stories. `components/archive/publish-dialog.tsx` owns selection and previews. Keep these cards in any future logo, typography or palette migration, alongside site, email and Telegram artwork. Exports respect the photo rotation; whole-photo fit is the default, and crop-to-fill is explicit.

Desktop headers use 16px edge gutters independently of the page content. Profile, appearance and sign-out boxes share a 52px height. The collection caption is centered against the entire header, while compact desktop widths constrain the profile label and gaps to prevent overlap. The phone header keeps its existing 64px navigation layout.

On desktop, the guest badge sits beneath the centered collection caption; neither label competes with the notification and account controls.

Dashboard covers are a shared brand surface for personal and followed trips. Keep the archive artwork while a preview is unavailable. Personal cards use the viewer's favorite photos within their access window; followed cards use only the owner's guest-visible favorites. Load a bounded deck through the scoped preview queue, start at a random frame, and crossfade every eight seconds over 280ms. Stop cycling offscreen, in hidden tabs and under reduced motion; no playback button. Publication exports accept only favorite, guest-visible still photos and explain the star action when empty.
