# QuestKeeps

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People keeping personal travel memories and sharing selected trips with friends and fellow travellers. Confirmed by the user’s feature requests and docs/requirements.md.

## Product Purpose

Bring photos, routes, places, journal entries and the people in a trip into a lasting travel archive.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Yandex Disk stores original media. Owners approve sources and give participants access by date. Guest links grant access to the whole published trip and can be revoked. Friendship requires explicit acceptance and never grants trip access. Own friends/requests are private by default (conservative implementation choice pending any future visibility preference). Profiles have separate display name and username. Russian/English and light/dark appearances remain supported. New request: standalone profile, friends on the home/profile pages, and About.

## Voice and Assets

QuestKeeps name and existing QK mark. Plain, warm Russian/English. No invented social statistics or public claims about a completed security audit.
